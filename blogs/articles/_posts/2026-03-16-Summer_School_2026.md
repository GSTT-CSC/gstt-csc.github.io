<span class="dropcap">F</span>ollowing the success of last year’s sold-out event, the Clinical Scientific Computing (CSC) team at Guy’s and St Thomas’ NHS Foundation Trust (GSTT) is delighted to announce the return of the CSC AI Summer School.

This will be a five-day event held at Guy’s Hospital, London, beginning on Monday 6 July 2026. Attendance in person is required.

Thirty-five attendees will learn about the current regulatory landscape, including software as a medical device, AI as a medical device, and quality management systems; data management; machine learning operations; and evaluation of commercial AI products.

The summer school is aimed at operational NHS staff who are likely to help implement AI solutions into clinical pathways, including clinical scientists, data analysts, and technically minded clinicians. 
For more information, and to apply for a place, click [here](https://www.guysandstthomasevents.co.uk/courses/csc-ai-summer-school-2026/).
