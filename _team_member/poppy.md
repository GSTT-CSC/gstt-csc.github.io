### Bio
Poppy joined the NHS through the NHS Graduate Management Training scheme, and has experience in non-clinical NHS roles. This includes operational service management and outpatient transformation roles at Homerton Healthcare NHS Foundation Trust, as well as communications and project management at the South East Genomic Medicine Service at Guy's and St Thomas'. Her interest and experience in AI began in her role with the NHS Genomic AI Network. 

### CSC Team Role
Poppy is the programme manager for the NHS Fellowship in Clinical AI, responsible for day-to-day operational management and delivery, as well as co-developing strategy and growth projects.
