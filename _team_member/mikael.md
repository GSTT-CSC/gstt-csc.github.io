### Bio
Mikaël has an academic background in medical physics. He earned his PhD in physics in 2021 at Université de Montréal, working on applications of spectral computed tomography in radiotherapy. He joined University College London in 2021 as a Marie-Curie research fellow, working on the development of deep-learning based methodologies for digital pathology, ion imaging and X-ray imaging. In 2025, he work at the Royal Marsden as a research imaging physicist before joining the Guy's and St Thomas' Trust as a Senior Clinical Scientist in Artificial Intelligence in March 2026.

### CSC Team Role
Mikaël's main focus will be on the development of the MLOps infrastructure and contribute to model training.
