### Bio
Saad completed an MEng in Biomedical Engineering at King’s College London, where his main projects focused on statistical shape analysis of the foetal cortex and aorta. In the summer of 2025, he completed an internship at The Alan Turing Institute, contributing to CVD-Net, a large-scale research initiative focused on cardiovascular digital twins.

### CSC Team Role
Saad joined the team in September 2026 as an STP trainee in Clinical Scientific Computing. He is particularly interested in the translation of software, machine learning and AI into healthcare, and is excited to develop his understanding of the complexities involved in taking computational models from development through to clinical deployment.
