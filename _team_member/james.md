### Bio
[James](https://www.jamesclarkross.co.uk) joined the CSC team as a Senior Clinical Scientist in February 2026, arriving with over ten years of experience at five different NHS trusts. He graduated from the [NHS Scientist Training Programme](/stp.html) in 2016, after being recruited onto the scheme in 2013 and specialising in Medical Physics (Imaging with Ionising Radiation). Not only does he bring a wealth of clinical knowledge to the table, he is an experienced researcher and has a PhD in philosophy.

### CSC Team Role
James's main roles include AI ethics, staff training, research, and website management.
