### Bio
Anthony joined the CSC team in September 2025 as a Trainee Clinical Scientist in Clinical Scientific Computing as part of the Scientist Training Programme. Before joining the team he completed a medical degree with neuroscience intercalation at the University of Cambridge and worked as an Academic Foundation Doctor in North London with a focus in Population Health Data Science.

### CSC Team Role
Anthony is keen on combining his front-line clinical experience with his interest in how computing related skills can be used to improve patient care, and hopes to develop his technical skills through contributing to projects which have tangible outcomes.
